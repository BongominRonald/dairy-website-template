Our Farmstead Story

Cultivating Purity From Pasture To Your Table

Founded in 2001, Milky Dairy Farm started with a modest herd of 20 Jersey cows and a stubborn commitment: never compromise on pasture grazing, animal welfare, or pure unadulterated milk flavor. Today, we nurture over 2,600 healthy cows across 1,200 acres of herbal clover meadows.

Holistic Agriculture
Clover rotational grazing restores topsoil minerals and builds healthy cows naturally.
Fresh Batch Bottling
Gentle low-temperature vat pasteurization locks in beneficial enzymes and sweet creaminess.
